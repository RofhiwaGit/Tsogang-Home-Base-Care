About Tsogang Home Base Care

Established on June 4, 2008, Tsogang Home Base Care is a non-profit organization dedicated to addressing the needs of elderly individuals (aged 60+) and disabled persons residing in underprivileged areas. Guided by the Constitution of South Africa, our mission is to provide compassionate and comprehensive home-based care, bringing hope, safety, stability, and humanity to our community.

  • Home visits by trained care workers
  • Assistance with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, etc.)
  • Wound care and chronic disease monitoring
  • Medication management and appointment support
  • Transportation and mobile clinic services

Bringing care directly to your home

Our Mission & Vision

Our aim is to bring hope, safety, dignity, and human rights to those suffering from chronic illnesses through home visits and mobile clinic services. Tsogang Health Facility promotes better health and wellness within the community by employing professional care workers who assist vulnerable individuals and refer them to necessary clinics and hospitals.

Our Vision

Bringing hope, safety, dignity, and human rights to the elderly and disabled in underprivileged communities.

Our Mission

Promote better health and wellness within the community through home-based care and mobile clinics.

Objective

Employ community nurses to visit patients in their homes, provide care, and facilitate referrals to clinics and hospitals.
