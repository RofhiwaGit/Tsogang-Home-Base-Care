Care That Starts at Home

Home-based care ensures that elderly and chronically ill individuals receive compassionate, continuous support in a familiar environment. This approachpreserves dignity, emotional well-being, and independence.

  • Comfort & dignity at home
  • Reduced hospital visits
  • Personalized care plans
  • Family involvement
  • Early health detection
  • Community-based support

Committed to Bringing Hope & Care

Our Services

We offer a range of services tailored to the needs of our elderly beneficiaries, including:

  • Assistance with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, etc.) and clothing care
  • Home visits by trained care workers
  • Wound care and monitoring of chronic diseases
  • Management of medication and appointments for medical care
  • Transportation to healthcare facilities for beneficiaries without ID
  • Delivering medication door-to-door
  • Adverse and monitoring exercise programs
  • Workshops and counselling families about chronic diseases
  • 180 women beneficiaries supported
  • 70 men beneficiaries supported
